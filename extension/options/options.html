LinkedIn Jobs Curator

Where your data lives

Rules, the application log, and the dismiss log are stored locally in this browser (browser.storage.local). Manage them from the panel on the right edge of any LinkedIn jobs page.

To move data between browsers, use Settings → Backup in that panel to export JSON or CSV, then import it on the other machine.

Google Sheets sync

Configured from the panel itself, not here — open Settings → Backup on any LinkedIn jobs page to connect a spreadsheet. See SETUP.md in the repo for the one-time Google Cloud steps needed before that page will work.
